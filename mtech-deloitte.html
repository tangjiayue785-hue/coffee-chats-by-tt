Consulting Event Notes

MTech Deloitte

UniMelb · 14 August 2026. Interview practice, case work, presentation lessons and networking takeaways.

Behavioural interview

Questions asked

  • Conflict
  • Being assigned a task with limited information
  • Why consulting, or specifically technology consulting
  • Multitasking
  • Managing and applying feedback

What I learned

  • Use “I”, not only “we”, so the interviewer can identify your individual contribution.
  • Use STAR to structure examples.
  • Draw on different experiences where possible.
  • Explain what you personally did to demonstrate the skill.
  • A brief summary at the end can make the answer easier to follow.
Best practice: use genuine experiences whenever possible. If you have not faced an exact scenario, use the closest real example and explain your reasoning rather than inventing a story.

General consulting notes

  • Year 1: explore early-entry opportunities such as MTech.
  • Year 2: consider internship or vacationer programs.
  • Year 3: graduate roles may become available.
  • Throughout university: do case competitions, attend events and build your network.
  • Practise behavioural interviews and case studies regularly using peers, videos and AI tools.

Group case interview

  • Split the task and assign roles at the beginning.
  • Prepare the introduction and conclusion deliberately.
  • Use data to support recommendations.
  • Consider available time and resources.
  • Make solutions specific and, where useful, creative.
  • Use confident body language and eye contact.
  • Introduce team members or use explicit handovers to keep the presentation cohesive.
  • Show enthusiasm for both the client and the recommendation.
  • Demonstrate in the introduction that you understand the client’s problem.
  • Break solutions into stages where appropriate and use feedback from each stage to shape the next.

Networking takeaways

Ninka Yu — Consultant, Functional + Technical Business Analyst @ Deloitte

Presentation: confidence and enthusiasm are as important as the content itself. Focus not only on what you present, but how you deliver it.

Abrar Yusuf — Senior Consultant @ KPMG

Interview: after explaining the theory or approach, spend more time expanding on relevant experiences. Structure each response so the interviewer can follow it easily.

Ananda Chu — Senior Test Engineer @ Deloitte

Experience: case competitions, clubs and societies can all build useful skills. Even organising events can demonstrate relevant capabilities. In team presentations, smooth handovers matter.
